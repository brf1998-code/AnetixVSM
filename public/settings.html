When a request arrives

System review

Now and then, not every day: the AI reads one whole tool, its feedback and build history and the live data, then files what it would change as held recommendations on the board's Reviewing column. You approve them one by one, or all together, or decline them. Nothing is built until you say so.

Test floor

Every tool together on data that is not the floor's. Walk around in it, try things, print a label; nothing there is real. A build gets its test campaign here before it goes on the floor. Anetix switches it on for you.

Your data

Everything your company put into Anetix, as one file: each tool's records, your requests and their answers, the answers to new-tool questions, attached photos and files, uploads, labels printed and your people. The tools' code is part of Anetix and is not in it.

Connected AI apps

An AI app your company already uses can connect to this company's Anetix. It reads your tools, how each one works, what changed and the requests on the board, and it can put a request on the board for you to review. It never changes a tool or a record, and nothing it files is read or built until a manager reviews it on the board.
